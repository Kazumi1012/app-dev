# My Favorite TV Series

This repository contains information about my favorite television show, styled completely with elements from the Markdown Cheat Sheet.

## Overview

The series follows the dizzying crisis and miraculous rekindling of love between a small-town lawyer and the haughty heiress of a massive department store conglomerate.

> "I thought marrying her was the success of my life. But it turned out to be a crisis."
> — Baek Hyun-woo

---

## Basic Show Facts

Here is some basic information about the series:

- **Title:** Queen of Tears (눈물의 여왕)
- **Genre:** Romance, Comedy, and Melodrama
- **Platform:** Netflix / tvN
- **Release Year:** 2024

You can find more details on the [Official IMDb Page](https://imdb.com).
